Hi friend, welcome to Indian Tax Calculator India!

Tax filing can feel scary because of big words. I will explain everything like a simple story using clear analogies so you feel confident and in control.

Think of your tax like this: Every year you earn money (your income). The government asks for a report card called ITR. They calculate tax on the "final bill" after you show all your discount receipts (deductions and exemptions). Some tax is already cut automatically (TDS). We just need to report correctly and see the full picture together.

Step 1: Gather Your Papers

Form 16 from every employer
This is your official salary report card. Look for "Gross Salary" in Part B — this is the main number we need.
Annual Information Statement (AIS) and Form 26AS
These are the government's own passbook showing interest, dividends, and tax already deducted.
How to open them — step by step

Official website: https://www.incometax.gov.in

  1. Login with PAN and password.
  2. Go to Services → Annual Information Statement (AIS).
  3. Select FY 2025-26.

Why it matters: Banks report your interest and dividends here. Always check it.

Step 3: New Tax Regime vs Old Tax Regime

New Tax Regime
Like a simple fixed-price thali — lower rates, fewer choices, but straightforward. Standard Deduction ₹75,000 + Rebate u/s 87A up to ₹60,000.
Old Tax Regime
Like a full buffet — pick many dishes (Section 80C ₹1.5 lakh, HRA, etc.). Standard Deduction ₹50,000 + Rebate u/s 87A up to ₹12,500.
Special savings boxes: PPF, ELSS, life insurance, home loan principal, tuition fees. Put money here for a big discount on your tax bill.
If you receive House Rent Allowance and live in rented house, you can reduce your taxable income. Keep rent receipts ready.

Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Not checking AIS and missing interest/dividend
  • Claiming deductions without proofs
  • Forgetting salary from previous employer
Smart Ways to Save Tax
  • Max out Section 80C (₹1.5 lakh) before 31 March
  • Claim HRA with proper rent receipts
  • Buy health insurance under Section 80D
