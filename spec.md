# Product Specification Document

**Product Name:** Income Tax Calculator India  
**Version:** 1.0
**Target Period:** FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27  
**Document Date:** April 2026  

## 1. Why This Tool Was Made (Purpose & Vision)

Most first-time taxpayers in India struggle with complex tax jargon such as “Taxable Income”, “New Tax Regime vs Old Tax Regime”, “Rebate under Section 87A”, “Section 80C”, “HRA exemption”, “AIS”, and “Form 26AS”.

The official government portal and most existing calculators assume users already understand these concepts. This results in confusion, missed deductions, filing mistakes, anxiety, and last-minute stress.

**IncomeTaxCalculatorIndia** was created to solve this problem by building **one single, unified, highly educational web page** that:

- Teaches real official tax terms using simple language, strong analogies, and practical examples
- Calculates tax accurately while explaining every step clearly
- Feels like a patient, friendly teacher guiding a complete beginner
- Helps users go from “I don’t understand anything” to “I understand my tax and I’m ready to file confidently”

**Core Vision:**  
Make Indian Income Tax simple, friendly, and educational so first-time taxpayers feel confident instead of scared.

## 2. Target Audience

- First-time salaried individuals (age 22–35)
- People who changed jobs during the financial year
- Beginners with salary + interest + dividend + simple rental income
- Anyone who finds tax forms and technical terms confusing

## 3. Key Objectives

- Use correct official tax terms but explain them deeply with relatable analogies and examples
- Provide visible help text and rich info buttons for better learning
- Show clear, unbiased comparison between New Tax Regime and Old Tax Regime
- Guide users on where to get correct data (Form 16, AIS, bank statements, etc.)
- Deliver accurate tax calculation with a step-by-step narrative
- Reduce common mistakes through warnings and practical tips
- Build user confidence with warm, encouraging language

## 4. Core Features

### Educational & Guided Experience
- 5-step clear progress bar with friendly navigation
- Strong, memorable analogies throughout (fixed-price thali vs buffet, special savings boxes, discount receipts, etc.)
- Visible inline help text under important fields
- Info (i) buttons with deeper explanations, real-life examples, practical tips, and common mistakes
- Warm, reassuring, and non-judgmental tone (“You’re doing great!”, “This is normal for first-timers”)

### Document Guidance
- Detailed explanation of Form 16 (Part A vs Part B)
- Step-by-step instructions on how to access AIS and Form 26AS with direct official link
- Emphasis on checking interest and dividend income in AIS

### Income Input Section
- Gross Salary (with support for multiple employers/job changes)
- Interest Income, Dividend Income, Rental Income
- Age group selection
- Real-time live preview that updates as user types

### New Tax Regime vs Old Tax Regime
- Visual side-by-side comparison with strong analogies
- Section 80C (₹1,50,000 limit clearly shown with list of common investments)
- HRA Exemption field with practical guidance and tips
- Section 80D (Health Insurance)
- Live tax impact preview when switching regimes

### Results – “Your Tax Story” Page
- Narrative-style summary titled “Your Tax Story This Year”
- Big highlighted “Recommended Regime” with exact ₹ savings amount
- Step-by-step breakdown: Gross Income → Deductions/Exemptions → Taxable Income → Final Tax
- Side-by-side regime comparison
- Common mistakes section
- Legit ways to save tax section
- Clear “Next Steps” checklist with direct link to incometax.gov.in

### Technical Features
- Accurate AY 2026-27 tax slabs and rules for both regimes
- Correct Standard Deduction (₹75,000 in New Regime, ₹50,000 in Old Regime)
- Proper handling of Rebate under Section 87A
- 4% Health & Education Cess
- Age-based exemption in Old Regime
- Fully self-contained single HTML + CSS + JavaScript file (works offline)
- Mobile-friendly and responsive design

### Safety & Guardrails
- Friendly warnings for multiple jobs and Advance Tax situations
- Clear guidance when the user may need ITR-2 or a professional CA
- Strong disclaimer at the bottom of the tool

## 5. Technology

- Single self-contained HTML + CSS + JavaScript file
- No server or backend required
- Works completely offline after saving the file
- Easy to share and distribute

## 6. Domain & Branding

- **Primary Domain:** incometaxcalculatorindia.com
- **Displayed Name in Tool:** IncomeTaxCalculatorIndia
- **Tagline:** “Your friendly Indian Income Tax calculator & teacher — explains everything simply”

## 7. Success Criteria

- Users complete the full flow comfortably in under 10–12 minutes
- Users clearly understand New vs Old Regime and why one is better for their situation
- High satisfaction with clarity, friendliness, and educational value
- Significant reduction in confusion around common tax concepts and documents
